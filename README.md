## ✨ What gemini-router Does For You

gemini-router is a powerful yet easy-to-use tool that acts as a smart scheduler for your AI commands. Here's what it handles automatically:

### 🔄 Smart Account Rotation
Tired of hitting "rate limit exceeded" errors? gemini-router knows which of your accounts have quota available and automatically routes your requests to the right one. No more manual switching between accounts!

### ⚖️ Load Balancing
If you run multiple Gemini tasks at once, gemini-router spreads the work evenly across all your accounts. This means faster processing and no single account getting overloaded.

### 🛡️ Adaptive Rate Limiting
The application constantly monitors your usage and adapts in real-time. It slows down when needed and speeds up when possible, keeping you within safe limits without slowing your work unnecessarily.

### 🔁 Automatic Retries
If a request fails due to a temporary issue, gemini-router doesn't give up. It smartly retries with the right timing, saving you from watching failures all day.

### 📊 SQLite Coordination
Your data is managed locally in an efficient SQLite database. This keeps everything organized and fast, even when you're running many tasks simultaneously.

### 📎 Attachments and Artifacts Support
gemini-router handles files and generated outputs properly, making sure everything you create or upload gets to the right place.
